Interpretation limits

  • PMV does not establish an individual occupant’s actual acceptability.
  • Allocation represents ideal managed matching under known inputs; spontaneous seating may not attain it.
  • The fixed environment excludes occupancy-driven thermal loads, airflow and radiant changes.
  • Deployed-CBE agreement, algorithm tests and field validity are distinct evidence levels.
