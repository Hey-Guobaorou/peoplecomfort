Interpretation boundary

Results depend on declared scores, capacity, access, scenarios and allocation rule. Variable total capacity changes resource use without a cost penalty here. Priority first-fit is a declared operating rule, not a forecast of spontaneous choice. Field outcomes and plan geometry need separate evidence.
