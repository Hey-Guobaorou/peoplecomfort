Spatial alternatives

One schematic, three zones, 12 simultaneous places in every design. No seat geometry is assumed.

Decision rules

Baseline selects the smallest S0 loss. Robust selects the smallest maximum development regret across S0–S2. Holdout mixes do not choose the primary design.

Loss in the edited S0 mix

Mean weighted loss after the best complete assignment under each design’s capacity and access constraints.

Design × composition

An infeasible result means not all users can be assigned. Lower is better; values are model scores, not percentages of dissatisfied people.

What this demonstrates

Changing demand or weights can change which capacity distribution performs best. The package reports full assignments, profile burdens, input provenance and holdout scores. This demo keeps its scores fixed across designs; when a real design changes the environment, supply design-specific scores or rerun the thermal model.
